## About Dragonkind

**Dragonkind** puts players into the Threshing, where every decision can affect what happens next.

Your choices may lead to:

- 🐉 Dragon encounters
- ⚔️ Dangerous confrontations
- 🔥 Bonding opportunities
- 💀 Failed or fatal routes
- 🎲 Unexpected outcomes
- 🗺️ Alternative paths through the Threshing

Players naturally want to know which choices lead to particular dragons, but community reports can sometimes produce different outcomes from similar routes.

That's why this project focuses on **player-reported evidence and route mapping** rather than presenting every sequence as a guaranteed answer.

# 🐉 Dragonkind Tail Types

Dragon color is only part of your result.

The community guide also tracks five **Dragonkind tail types**:

- **Clubtail**
- **Daggertail**
- **Swordtail**
- **Morningstartail**
- **Scorpiontail**

Together with the six tracked dragon colors, these tail types create **30 color-and-tail combinations** to discover.

For example, players may encounter dragons belonging to combinations such as:

```text

Green + Swordtail

Orange + Morningstartail
```

The goal of the community database is to document these results alongside the choices and routes that produced them.

# 🔍 Dragonkind Answers & Choices

Players frequently look for **Dragonkind answers** or the "correct" choices for reaching a specific dragon.

However, community reports suggest that Dragonkind should not always be treated as a game with one universal answer key.

A route reported by one player may not necessarily guarantee the same result for another player.

For this reason, Dragonkind.world records the relationship between:

```text
CHOICE
   ↓
ROUTE
   ↓
ENCOUNTER
   ↓
RESULT
```

rather than labeling every reported path as a guaranteed solution.

The community data can help investigate questions such as:

- What are the best Dragonkind choices?
- Which Dragonkind route should I take?
- How do I get a dragon in Dragonkind?
- How do I get a black dragon in Dragonkind?
- Which route can lead to a blue dragon?
- What choices can lead to death?
- Why did I get a different result from the same route?
- Are some Dragonkind outcomes random?
- What dragon colors are available?
- What Dragonkind tail types exist?

If you're searching for a specific outcome, start by comparing multiple community reports rather than relying on a single route.

# ⚫ How to Get a Black Dragon in Dragonkind

The **Black Dragon** is one of the Dragonkind outcomes players commonly search for.

However, a player-reported Black Dragon route should be treated as evidence rather than a guaranteed answer.

When researching a Black Dragon route, compare:

- The player's full choice sequence
- The encounter immediately before bonding
- Reported deaths along similar routes
- Other players who followed the same choices
- Different tail types reported with Black Dragons
- Conflicting outcomes from similar runs

As more reports are collected, repeated patterns become easier to identify.

# ❓ Dragonkind FAQ

## What is Dragonkind?

Dragonkind is an interactive experience centered around the **Threshing**, where players make decisions, survive encounters, and attempt to bond with a dragon.

## Is there a Dragonkind guide?

Yes. **Dragonkind.world** provides an unofficial community Dragonkind game guide covering Threshing routes, dragon types, player choices, and reported outcomes.

👉 [Visit the Dragonkind Game Guide](https://dragonkind.world/)

## Is there a Dragonkind route map?

Yes. The **Dragonkind Threshing Route Atlas** organizes player-reported routes so different choices and outcomes can be compared.

👉 [Explore Dragonkind Threshing Routes](https://dragonkind.world/)

## What dragon colors are in Dragonkind?

The community guide currently tracks:

**Black, Blue, Green, Red, Orange, and Brown dragons.**

## What tail types are in Dragonkind?

Tracked Dragonkind tail types include:

**Clubtail, Daggertail, Swordtail, Morningstartail, and Scorpiontail.**

## How many Dragonkind dragon combinations are there?

With six tracked colors and five tracked tail types, there are **30 possible color-and-tail combinations** represented by the current classification system.

## How do I get a Black Dragon in Dragonkind?

Community reports can help identify routes associated with Black Dragon encounters, but individual reports should not automatically be treated as guaranteed outcomes.

Use the **Dragonkind Threshing Route Atlas** to compare multiple reported runs.

## Are Dragonkind routes guaranteed?

No route documented from community reports should automatically be assumed to guarantee a particular result.

Different players may report different outcomes after similar choices.

## Where can I find Dragonkind answers?

Rather than maintaining a simple universal answer key, Dragonkind.world collects player-reported choices and outcomes so you can compare routes and identify recurring patterns.

## Where can I learn how to play Dragonkind?

Start with the **[How to Play Dragonkind](https://dragonkind.world/how-to-play-dragonkind)** guide.
