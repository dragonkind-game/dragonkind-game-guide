FAQ

Dragonkind questions players ask.

What is Dragonkind?

Dragonkind is an interactive experience centered around the Threshing, where players make choices, face dangerous encounters, and may ultimately bond with a dragon.

Where can I find a Dragonkind game guide?

Dragonkind.world provides an unofficial community guide covering Threshing routes, dragon types, player-reported choices, deaths, and bonding outcomes.

Is there a Dragonkind route map?

Yes. The Dragonkind Threshing Route Atlas organizes community reports so players can compare routes and outcomes across different runs.

What dragon colors are tracked?

The community guide currently tracks Black, Blue, Green, Red, Orange, and Brown dragons.

Are Dragonkind routes guaranteed?

Player reports can conflict, so community-documented routes should be treated as evidence rather than guaranteed outcomes.
